# AI Whisperers — Logo Pack (SVG)

This pack includes:
- `logo_stacked.svg` — primary lockup (mark above wordmark).
- `logo_horizontal.svg` — horizontal lockup.
- `logo_mark_only.svg` — symbol only.
- `logo_stacked_mono.svg` — single-color variant.

## Color palette
- Primary Blue: #1B3CFF
- Blue Accent: #3BA6FF
- Violet Accent: #8B5CF6
- Text: #222222

## Notes
- The hexagon outline uses rounded joins and caps to get a friendly tech feel without sharp corners.
- The inner triad spiral is purposefully subtle (not an overt triskelion) to avoid cultural overtones.
- The wave strokes (gradient) suggest “whispered” AI signals becoming profit-oriented flow.
- Typography uses generic system sans-serif fallbacks; feel free to replace with your brand font.
